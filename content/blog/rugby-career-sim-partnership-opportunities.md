One of the goals the store listing suggests for Pro Rugby Career Sport Sim is "simply learning what the match numbers mean". That modest ambition is why we think the game suits community programmes run by rugby clubs and unions for people new to the sport, as well as publishers looking for an approachable rugby title. Its page on this site also lists among its audience players who prefer short sessions that build into a longer career, and anyone who likes reading match stats to see what improved. Reign Creative LLC owns the game and is open to partnerships, licensing, publishing and acquisition.

## A way into the sport

The Google Play listing describes a game about one player rather than a club. You create the athlete with a portrait and a position, then decide what kind of player they become: one who carries the ball, one who makes the tackles, or one whose value is in supporting the players around them. Training turns that decision into skills for the role, and the listing recommends turning your next goal into a training plan rather than improving everything at once.

The rhythm, in the listing's words, is prepare, follow the action, review. Matches are simulated on the field, and you follow your own player through them. Each new season adds another run of matches. It is free to download, supported by ads, and Google Play lists in-app purchases. The app page's FAQ adds that you do not need to know rugby well to play.

## Carries, metres and tackles

The stats the game tracks are the ones that describe an individual's contribution to a team sport. A performance reads as more than the final score, and the numbers are what you use afterwards to decide what to work on.

A worked example a programme leader might use: a simulated player who makes 12 carries for 60 metres averages 60 ÷ 12 = 5 metres per carry. Comparing that figure across matches is a simple way to show newcomers what the carry count is measuring. For the real-world mechanics behind a tackle, the site's guide to [the breakdown, tackle, ruck and maul](/blog/rugby-breakdown-tackle-ruck-maul/) is a useful companion.

## Community programmes: careful, practical ideas

A club's community or development team might use the game as a low-pressure introduction to positions and roles. Possible arrangements, all subject to discussion:

- **A themed or co-branded edition** for a programme, where technically feasible, with fictional placeholder identity replaced by the club's own.
- **Introductory content** co-written with coaches, for instance explaining what each position's numbers mean, building on the site's overview of [rugby career paths](/blog/rugby-career-paths/).
- **Cross-promotion** to families and new members.

We would not present the game as coaching or as a substitute for playing. It is a career game in which you follow a simulated player.

## Publishers: a rugby pair

Reign Creative also publishes [24-0 Rugby Draft Pro League](/apps/rugby-draft-pro-league/), a squad-building and GM game. The app page's FAQ draws the line: this game is one player's career; the other is drafting and managing a whole squad. A publisher could take either or both into new markets, localise them, or co-develop further content. The player-facing [Pro Rugby Career Sport Sim guide](/blog/rugby-career-sim-guide/) and the [app page](/apps/rugby-career-sim/) describe the game as it stands.

## Limits worth stating

The app's page is clear that there is no squad or club management, and that matches are simulated rather than controlled phase by phase. It does not name real clubs, unions or competitions, and nothing in it is licensed from them; any use of a club's or union's marks would be a new arrangement under their own terms. We do not publish financial or player figures.

Write to ReignCreativeSupport@gmail.com or use the form on the [partnerships page](/partnerships/). A short note with your organisation and idea is enough to begin. ## Moving the app under a new account

For a sale, Google Play's [transfer help page](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6230247) sets out the route: compliant accounts and apps, downloaded reports, two active developer accounts and the target's registration transaction ID, then a request the target approves. The listing's users, ratings, reviews and statistics move across. For an app with in-app products, the buyer's account needs a payments profile; for an app with ads, AdMob and other ad SDKs are updated so traffic is credited to the buyer. Promotions are not carried over, though promo codes already issued should keep working.
