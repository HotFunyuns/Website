Will I get minutes, should I move clubs, what happens after an injury? Football Career Soccer XI Sim is built around questions like these, played out over one fictional footballer's career. Reign Creative LLC owns the game and is interested in talking with football academies, soccer media and publishers about partnerships, licensing, distribution or a sale.

## A career that an academy would recognise

The Google Play listing traces the career through youth football, academy, university football, turning professional, the senior years, a superstar spell, the veteran seasons, retirement and legacy. You create the footballer, choose one of six positions — goalkeeper, defender, midfielder, winger, forward or striker — then an archetype and a playing style, and develop attributes such as finishing, passing, dribbling, pace, strength, defending, vision, stamina and goalkeeping.

Playing time has to be earned. **Coach Trust**, in the listing's description, moves a player from rotation option to starter, key player and eventually club icon, and a newly started career can begin on the bench. Away from matches, transfers, contract talks, free agency, rivalries, relationships with teammates and coaches, injuries and comebacks, press conferences, social media, endorsements, international selection and milestones all respond to form. The site covers several of these topics for players, including [Coach Trust and playing time](/blog/coach-trust-and-playing-time/) and [press conferences in career mode](/blog/press-conferences-in-career-mode/).

Matches run through an interactive simulator that surfaces the key moments: pass, shoot, dribble, cross, press, tackle, make the run or try the risky ball. Attributes, position, energy, morale, the match situation and difficulty all weigh on the result. The game is free, carries ads and offers optional in-app purchases. Every club, competition and player is fictional.

## Ideas for academies and clubs

An academy's interest is likely to be conversational rather than technical: a game its players already understand, about choices they will face. Possible directions, each a matter for discussion:

- **A themed edition** carrying an academy's or community club's identity in place of fictional branding, where technically feasible.
- **Co-developed scenarios** about the academy-to-first-team step, written with coaches who know it. The site's explainer on [how soccer academies work](/blog/how-soccer-academies-work/) shows the real-world background such content would draw on.
- **Educational partnerships**, where the game is one talking point in a wider programme.

We would be careful with claims. The game does not train footballers and we would not market it as if it did. What it can do is give coaches and young players a shared, low-stakes example to talk through: a simulated transfer offer that promises a bigger club but fewer minutes, which the app page's FAQ describes, is the kind of dilemma an academy session might pick apart.

## Soccer media and sponsorship

The career's off-pitch layer — transfers, press conferences, endorsements — gives a media partner natural editorial hooks. Sponsored content or events would need to suit the game and its audience and would be agreed in writing. A partner should also know that the app's page on this site names goalkeepers among its audience, as players "who rarely get a dedicated career path", which may interest outlets that cover goalkeeping.

A worked example of a content series: following each of the six positions through the nine career phases the listing names would give 6 × 9 = 54 possible position-and-phase pairings, enough for a long-running feature without repeating one. It is an editorial idea, not a viewing projection.

## The Google Play side of a sale

Google's [app transfer help page](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6230247) describes the mechanics. Both developer accounts need to be registered and active; the original owner should download any bulk export, payout or earnings reports first, because those stay behind. Users, statistics, ratings, reviews, subscriptions and comments go with the app. Because this title sells in-app products, the buyer's account needs an active payments profile, and a default-currency difference would leave the app unpublished until the buyer checks prices and republishes. Advertising SDKs, AdMob included, have to be updated to credit the buyer's account.
