**In brief:** Hockey Career Sim 2027 is a one-player hockey career game from Reign Creative LLC. It plays offline, gives goalies a full career of their own, and uses only fictional leagues and teams. We are open to licensing it, distributing it through regional partners, publishing it with a partner, co-developing it, or selling it. Hockey programmes, regional distributors and publishers are the partners we have in mind. The rest of this page fills in the detail.

## One player's skates, start to finish

The Google Play listing has you pick a position and archetype — a scorer, a playmaker, a defensive leader or an elite netminder — and take that player from young prospect through the seasons that decide the career. On the ice you shoot, pass, defend, challenge the puck carrier or protect the net, and those choices move momentum. Off it, skating, shooting, passing, puck control, checking, positioning, stamina, awareness and goaltending develop through training.

Seasons bring schedules, standings, statistics, awards, playoffs and rivals, and roster spots have to be earned. Coaches, teammates, agents, media and rivals all react, alongside recovery, injuries, contracts and expectations. The game is free with ads and offers optional in-app purchases. For players, the [Hockey Career Sim 2027 guide](/blog/hockey-career-sim-guide/) and the [app page](/apps/hockey-career-sim/) go further.

## Goaltenders get a full career

The app's page on this site counts "goalie players under-served by roster-management games" among its audience, and its FAQ says goaltender is a full career path with its own attributes and decisions, "not a variation on a skater". That gives a hockey programme or a goaltending coach a specific reason to talk to us: co-developed goalie scenarios, a themed edition for a goalie camp, or cross-promotion to families of young netminders. The site's [hockey goaltending explainer](/blog/hockey-goaltending-explained/) covers the position itself. As always, the game is entertainment; we make no claim it trains anyone.

## Why offline play matters to a distributor

The listing describes the game as offline, so a season can continue without a connection. For a regional distributor that is a practical selling point: the game does not depend on a live service to run. Localisation would still be a project, since the listing does not describe language options, and a distributor's local knowledge of how hockey is followed in its region would shape the store copy.

A worked example of how a distributor might frame a launch: the listing names four positions (forward, centre, defenceman, goalie) and four archetypes. A launch campaign that featured each position-and-archetype pairing once would have 4 × 4 = 16 spotlights to rotate, two a week for eight weeks. Not every pairing may make sense in-game, which is exactly what a scoping call would check.

## Hockey programmes and publishers

For a youth or community hockey programme, the archetype system is a natural conversation piece about roles, linked to the site's [hockey career archetypes explainer](/blog/hockey-career-archetypes-explained/). For a publisher, the game pairs with our on-ice action title, [Hockey Career Pro League Game](/apps/hockey-career-pro-league/), giving a decision-led and a hands-on hockey career in one slate. New audiences may need the basics, such as [hockey rules for beginners](/blog/hockey-rules-for-beginners/).

## What a licence covers, and what it cannot

We can discuss licensing the software, its design and its presentation, including a branded or white-label edition where technically feasible. We cannot license league names, team identities or players, because the game uses none and we hold no such rights; the app's page confirms it is not affiliated with or licensed by any real league, club or athlete. A partner wanting real marks would need agreements with their owners. We do not publish prices, valuations, revenue or player counts.

## Raising a hockey licence with us

The [partnerships page](/partnerships/) has an inquiry form, or email ReignCreativeSupport@gmail.com. Introduce your organisation and the idea briefly; please hold back confidential, financial or personal information for a later stage, when it can be shared with qualified parties under NDA if appropriate. Every discussion is nonbinding and any licence or sale depends on due diligence and a signed agreement.

## If Hockey Career Sim 2027 changes owners

If the conversation becomes a sale, Google's [transfer help page](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6230247) applies. Points that matter for a game like this: Google lists integrated services, including Google Analytics, Firebase and Play game services, whose permissions and links have to be updated because they do not transfer automatically; for Play App Signing, the buyer can request a new upload key or a signing key upgrade if either side has concerns; a buyer with a different default currency would see an in-app-purchase app unpublished until prices are verified. Users, statistics, ratings and reviews move with the app. Approval by the receiving developer and a review by Google's support team complete the request.
