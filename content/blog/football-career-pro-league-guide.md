## Creating and training your player

You create a player whose skills match your style, then train **passing, running and defense**. The listing describes preparing for the draft and working to earn a role on a team, so the career begins before your first snap as a pro; [how the NFL draft works](/blog/how-does-the-nfl-draft-work/) explains the real event the game's draft echoes. Training and equipment feed player development, daily rewards give a reason to return, and trophies are the long-term goal.

## Offense, defense and the three camera views

The listing describes what you do in the action:

- **As a quarterback**, read the defence, find space for passes and attack openings on running plays.
- **On defense**, switch over to stop the other side.
- **Skill moves** appear in the store screenshots alongside offense and defense.

Three camera views, **Standard, Wide and Full Field**, change how much of the play you see. The listing says the framing is there to track passing options, spot running lanes and read defense across the field; [cover 2 versus cover 3](/blog/cover-2-vs-cover-3/) explains the shells you are reading.

The listing does not say which real coverages the game's defences use, so treat real-football reading as background rather than a guide to the engine. In the real game, as our coverage guide explains, the number in a coverage's name counts the defenders playing deep: two deep halves in Cover 2, three deep thirds in Cover 3, and every defender added deep is one fewer underneath.

## Career, season, commissioner and Team Play modes

Career is the core, but the listing names **season, commissioner and Team Play** modes too, so you can look at the league from a team's side. You can study your **depth chart**, follow the draft and manage development; [depth charts explained](/blog/depth-charts-explained/) explains why a depth chart decides who plays.

You can save from the pause menu and keep **separate career saves** for different player journeys.

The listing frames the extra modes as ways to approach the game from a different angle: studying the depth chart, following the draft and deciding how your team fits together. The listing does not describe what commissioner mode controls; [what a sports commissioner does](/blog/what-does-a-sports-commissioner-do/) explains the real-world job of that title.

## A first season in Football Career Pro League

The goal of a first season is to learn what the game rewards before chasing records. The listing frames the career as preparing for the draft and finding your role on a team, so the early aim is to learn the controls and the camera before chasing results.

1. **Learn the controls in quick play** before a career game.
2. **Use the Wide or Full Field camera** while learning to read coverage.
3. **Pick training that fits your position**; [football positions explained](/blog/football-positions-explained-for-drafting/) shows what each needs.
4. **Check the depth chart** to see what stands between you and more snaps.
5. **Keep a second save** for experiments.
