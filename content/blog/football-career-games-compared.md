Reign Creative makes four American football games for Android, and each puts you in a different seat: playing the downs, making a quarterback's decisions, building a roster, or running the league. This comparison draws on their Google Play listings and their pages on this site to show which suits you; for a primer on the sport itself, see [how American football works](/blog/how-does-american-football-work/).

## The four games at a glance

| | Football Career Pro League | Football Career Sim 2027 | Football Franchise GM League | Football League Commissioner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| You are | A player on the field | A quarterback, through decisions | The general manager | The commissioner |
| Games | Arcade 11-on-11, played with controls | Drives resolved through your calls | Simulated from your roster | Simulated |
| Positions | Offense and defense | Quarterback only | Whole roster | Whole league |
| Players | Your created player | All fictional | The game's own ratings of real players' careers | Real players from football's eras, per its screenshots, with its own ratings |
| Ads / in-app purchases | Ads; none listed | Ads; purchases listed | Ads; purchases listed | Ads; none listed |

## Football Career Pro League: play the downs

The listing describes a player career combined with on-field action: create a player, train passing, running and defense, prepare for the draft and earn a role. On the field you read the defence as a quarterback, attack openings on running plays and switch to defense. It also names season, commissioner and Team Play modes, and the screenshots describe a 17-game season. [Its guide](/blog/football-career-pro-league-guide/) covers it in depth.

## Football Career Sim 2027: the quarterback's decisions

[Football Career Sim 2027](/apps/football-career-sim/) puts you behind centre for a single career, and its page ties your calls to the score, your statistics, your coach's trust and the depth chart. You build a quarterback, throwing hand, archetype and ratings such as accuracy and awareness, and choose a style: pocket passer, scrambler, field general or dual-threat. The career runs through youth football, high school, college, combine preparation and the draft. On each drive you read the defence, pick the target, call audibles and manage the clock; around the field sit training, injuries, contracts and free agency. [The football career sim guide](/blog/football-career-sim-guide/) explains it.

## Football Franchise GM League: build a roster

[Football Franchise GM League](/apps/football-draft-gm-mode/) starts on the draft board: compare ratings, fill each slot and defend your roster in a simulated season. Draft rules change the problem: Unlimited Draft for comparing options, Limited Draft with no refreshes, Salary Cap Draft and Playoff Mode, plus Franchise and GM modes and a Daily Challenge. [How the NFL draft works](/blog/how-does-the-nfl-draft-work/) explains the real event behind the idea.

## Football League Commissioner: run the league

[Football League Commissioner](/apps/football-league-commissioner/) is about the league office: rules, expansion and relocation, schedules, transaction approvals and salary-cap settings, with history kept in a Yearbook. You can simulate a game, a week, a season or the playoffs, follow favourite teams in observer mode, and try ideas in a sandbox or build a roster in Unlimited Draft. The store screenshots show a Commissioner's Office with league health and owner, player and fan scores beside a commissioner agenda. [Its guide](/blog/football-league-commissioner-guide/) explains the modes, and [what a sports commissioner does](/blog/what-does-a-sports-commissioner-do/) describes the real-world job of that title.

## Choosing by the seat you want

1. **You want to play on the field:** [Football Career Pro League](/apps/football-career-pro-league/).
2. **You love quarterback decisions and a career story:** Football Career Sim 2027.
3. **You enjoy roster puzzles and chasing a perfect record:** Football Franchise GM League.
4. **You want to shape a league:** Football League Commissioner.

The split is between doing and deciding. Only Football Career Pro League has on-field control; the other three resolve games by simulation. Of those, the quarterback sim keeps you inside one player's drives, the GM game judges a roster you drafted (its Salary Cap Draft, per its page, rewards spending well and exposes the units you skimped on), and the commissioner game makes decisions that affect every team.

## What each of the four leaves out

The simulation games have no on-field play; Football Career Sim 2027 is quarterback-only; and Football Career Pro League simplifies the playbook compared with a full coaching simulation, as its page on this site notes. Ratings in the games using real players are each game's own estimates, and none is affiliated with any league, team, players' association or athlete.
