In Football Franchise GM League, every run is a draft, which makes it a natural fit for people whose hobby or beat is the draft itself. Reign Creative LLC owns and develops it, and we would like to hear from fantasy and draft communities, podcasts and newsletters, and sports media outlets who see a fit. Below are the questions such partners tend to ask, answered from the app's page on this site and its store listing.

## What happens in a run of Football Franchise GM League?

The Google Play listing describes a draft-board start: compare ratings, fill each slot with the lineup builder, and finish with a roster you are prepared to defend. The season simulator then decides whether the plan holds, with an unbeaten 20-0 record as the target. Draft rules change the puzzle. Unlimited Draft lets you keep comparing; Limited Draft removes refreshes so every pick counts; Salary Cap Draft rewards spending well; Playoff Mode puts the whole roster under pressure. Franchise Mode and GM Mode extend control past draft day.

Competitive and knowledge formats sit around that core: VS Computer, Battle and Tournament against an opponent, a Daily Challenge with a set goal, and **Gridiron IQ** tests. Seasons are simulated; nobody plays the downs. A full player-facing explanation is in the [football draft and GM guide](/blog/football-draft-gm-guide/), and the app page is [/apps/football-draft-gm-mode/](/apps/football-draft-gm-mode/).

## Has the name changed, and does that matter to a partner?

Yes, and briefly. The app's page on this site says the app was listed on Google Play as 20-0 Football Draft & GM Mode until its recent rename, and the store description still uses that name. A media partner writing about it, or a sponsor printing its name, should use the current title, Football Franchise GM League, and may want to mention the former one so readers can find it.

## Which community formats does it already support?

Several features translate directly into things a community can run:

| Feature | Community use |
| --- | --- |
| Daily Challenge | A shared daily prompt members attempt and compare |
| Limited Draft (no refreshes) | A "one shot" draft night where nobody can reroll |
| Salary Cap Draft | Debates about value, linked to how real caps work |
| Tournament and Battle | Bracket-style competition between members' rosters |
| Gridiron IQ | Quiz segments for a podcast or stream |

These describe existing modes; organised leagues, prizes or sponsored branding would be scoped separately. A newsletter could pair a draft night with background reading such as [how the NFL salary cap works](/blog/how-does-the-nfl-salary-cap-work/) or the site's look at [whether any team has gone 20-0](/blog/has-any-team-gone-20-0/).

A worked example for a bracket: a single-elimination tournament among 16 community members needs 16 − 1 = 15 matchups to crown a winner, spread over 4 rounds because 2⁴ = 16. Organisers can plan a draft night around that.

## What about real players and official data?

Sports media are likely to raise this, so it deserves a plain answer. The draftable names come from real careers, but the ratings attached to them are the game's own estimates rather than official statistics. No league, club, union of players or individual athlete has endorsed or licensed the game, and none is connected with it. In practice that means a media partner should describe the ratings as the game's estimates, should not suggest any official backing, and should expect that league logos, team marks or a licensed statistics feed would need agreement from whoever owns them before they could appear anywhere near the product.

## How is the game funded?

It is free to download, supported by ads, with optional in-app purchases.

## How do we start, and what if we want to buy it?

Write through the form on the [partnerships page](/partnerships/) or email ReignCreativeSupport@gmail.com. A few sentences on who you are and what you have in mind is enough; leave confidential, financial or personal information out of a first message. We would arrange a call if there is a fit. All discussion is nonbinding, and any sponsorship, licence or sale depends on due diligence and terms both parties sign.

For an acquisition, Google Play's [transfer process](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/6230247) moves the app between developer accounts. The seller needs the receiving account's registration transaction ID, which Google says can be found by searching the target owner's inbox for "developer registration fee". Firebase projects are unlinked from the original Play Console account and linked to the target, and ad SDK integrations are updated. Promotions do not transfer, though previously issued promo codes should still work. What else is included in a sale is a matter for the written agreement.
