Brain games sit between entertainment, education and wellbeing. This overview answers three questions a partner from any of those areas would need settled — what is in the app, what can be said about it, and could it be bought or licensed — for [IQ Test & Brain Training Games](/apps/mental-math-memory-games/), which the app's page on this site says was listed on Google Play as Mental Math & Memory Games until its recent rename.

## What is inside the app

The listing describes several kinds of mental practice in one place:

| Area | What it includes |
| --- | --- |
| IQ-style test | Numerical reasoning, logic, patterns, vocabulary, working memory, spatial reasoning and processing speed, followed by practice recommendations |
| Brain games | Math, memory, language, logic, attention and speed, in quick play, daily, survival, boss and progression modes |
| Memory games | Digit span, sequences, grids, colours, letters, symbols and mixed recall |
| Logic and vocabulary | Analogies, sequences, conditional reasoning, deduction, synonyms, antonyms and context clues |
| Mental math | The four operations, percentages, ratios, estimation and money problems |
| Self-reflection | A personality questionnaire with original questions and a Big Five profile |
| Tracking | Daily challenges, XP, streaks, personal bests, a reaction time test and charts |

It is free to download, supported by ads, with optional in-app purchases.

## The claims boundary

Any partner in this category needs a firm rule on wording, and the listing provides one. It says **IQ test, personality test, memory test and other results are estimates based on app activities**, not medical, psychological, clinical, educational-placement, employment or professional diagnostic assessments. The app's page on this site goes further: **getting better at the app's games is not evidence of a general change in intelligence**. The personality test is offered **for self-reflection rather than diagnosis**.

So partnership material can talk about practice, variety, enjoyment and progress against a player's own history. It cannot promise sharper minds, protection against decline or anything clinical. That boundary protects users and partners alike.

## Partners who fit

**Education publishers.** The mental math, vocabulary and logic sets overlap with school skills. A publisher could explore licensing, co-developed question packs or a white-label version where technically feasible.

**Wellness publishers.** A daily-challenge habit fits wellbeing content about routine and leisure, presented honestly as games rather than therapy.

**Community programmes.** For libraries, community centres and clubs that run social puzzle sessions, sponsorship, cross-promotion or a co-branded session guide are possible, again framed as recreation.

## A worked example: a library puzzle club

A library runs a weekly hour-long puzzle club for ten adults. Each session opens with ten minutes of the daily challenge on members' own phones, followed by a paper logic puzzle discussed as a group, then a vocabulary game played in pairs. Over a term of 12 weeks, that is 120 minutes of app use per member and a lot of conversation. The library needs nothing from Reign Creative to do this, but a partnership could add printed guides, accessibility notes or themed packs. What the club would not do is chart members' "IQ" as a measure of improvement, because the app's own listing says its results are estimates, not assessments.

## Moving the app between Play accounts

When an app is sold, Google Play handles the move. Its Play Console help asks both sides to have **registered, active developer accounts** and to provide the registration transaction IDs, and it requires an **active payments profile** on the receiving account for apps with in-app products, which this app has. The help page lists what goes across, including **users, ratings, reviews and store listing information**, and what stays, such as **earnings reports and orders made before the transfer**. Promotions do not move, though previously issued promo codes should still work. Ad SDK integrations are updated afterwards so that ad traffic is credited to the correct account.
