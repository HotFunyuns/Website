## Partnership models for publishers

**Publishing and distribution.** A publisher with reach in markets the game does not yet serve could take on distribution, subject to each market's rules for simulated casino content.

**Localisation.** Fifty themes means a lot of text, symbols and sound to adapt. Localisation partners would review whether each theme travels, which symbols carry different meanings in different places, and how the game's no-real-money statements read in each language, since those statements must stay as clear after translation as they are in English.

**Co-developed themes.** New machines built with a publisher's art team, consistent with the existing volatility tiers.

**Licensing and white-label versions where technically feasible**, for publishers that want the engine and catalogue under their own brand.

A publisher planning a launch wants a simple lobby: one row per volatility tier. If the 50 machines were split evenly across three tiers, rows would hold 17, 17 and 16 machines (17 + 17 + 16 = 50).
