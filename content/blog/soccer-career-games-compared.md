Soccer has more than one kind of "career game". You can play the matches yourself, live a whole career through its key moments, or build a team and let the season play out. Reign Creative makes one of each for Android. This comparison uses each app's page on this site to help you pick.

## The three games at a glance

| | Football Career Soccer League | Football Career Soccer XI Sim | Pro Football GM Club Soccer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| You are | A player on the pitch | A player, through decisions | The squad builder |
| Matches | Played with controls | Interactive key moments | Simulated |
| Progress | Skills across a league season | Youth to retirement and legacy | Saved runs to beat |
| Players | Not described | All clubs, competitions and players fictional | A pool built from real players' careers, with the game's own estimated ratings |
| Ads / in-app purchases | Ads; none listed | Ads; purchases listed | Ads; purchases listed |

## Football Career Soccer League: play the match

[Football Career Soccer League](/apps/football-career-soccer-league/) is built around playing your own career on the pitch: follow one player in career mode, switch to team control for a wider view, or start a quick play game. Matches are played rather than menu-driven, and you check match stats after each one to set a target. You can also spectate. Its in-game title is Soccer Career Pro League. [Its guide](/blog/football-career-soccer-league-guide/) covers it in depth.

## Football Career Soccer XI Sim: live the career

[Football Career Soccer XI Sim](/apps/soccer-career-sim-xi/) is a career simulator from one player's point of view. You create a footballer, choose a position from goalkeeper to striker, pick an archetype and style, and develop attributes such as finishing, passing, pace and vision. The career runs through youth football, academy, university football, going pro, a superstar spell, the veteran years, retirement and legacy. Matches surface the key moments: pass, shoot, dribble, press, tackle or try the risky ball.

Around the pitch sit transfers, contracts, free agency, rivalries, injuries, press conferences, endorsements and international selection. **Coach Trust** decides whether you are a rotation player, a starter, a key player or a club icon, so a new career can begin on the bench; [coach trust and playing time](/blog/coach-trust-and-playing-time/) explains how to earn minutes. [The soccer career sim guide](/blog/soccer-career-sim-guide/) has more.

## Pro Football GM Club Soccer: draft the XI

[Pro Football GM Club Soccer](/apps/soccer-draft-gm-xi/) is a draft game. Each run offers fresh player choices; you pick across the pitch, balance the squad and finish with an XI you believe can survive a campaign. The simulation reports the season in wins, draws and losses, and a perfect record takes more than collecting the highest ratings. Modes include limited drafts, budget challenges, computer battles, tournaments and season modes, with club management adding formations, depth, transfers and contracts.

## Which should you pick?

1. **You want to play the football yourself:** Football Career Soccer League.
2. **You want a full career story, from academy to legacy:** Football Career Soccer XI Sim.
3. **You enjoy squad-building puzzles:** Pro Football GM Club Soccer.

The three also connect to different parts of the real sport. If you play the matches, [the offside rule](/blog/how-the-offside-rule-works/) is one of the laws behind attacking movement. The career sim's path from academy to professional contract has a real-world counterpart that [how soccer academies work](/blog/how-soccer-academies-work/) describes. The draft game is about squad balance: its page notes that position fit, player style and squad balance all feed into the result, so the highest-rated XI is not automatically the best one.

## Session length and pace

The games also differ in how much time each sitting asks for. The page for Pro Football GM Club Soccer says a draft and simulation fits comfortably into a short session, with club management there for a longer save. A match in Football Career Soccer League asks for your attention throughout, because you are controlling the play, though quick play keeps a session short. Football Career Soccer XI Sim surfaces key moments rather than every touch, and its career runs all the way from youth football to retirement and legacy. Choosing by the time you have is as reasonable as choosing by style.

## Limits to keep in mind

Football Career Soccer League's listing does not describe online multiplayer, and its in-game title differs from its store name. Soccer XI Sim's world is entirely fictional, and the GM game's ratings are its own estimates, not official statistics; neither is affiliated with any league, club, players' association or athlete. All three are among our [sports career games](/apps/category/sports-gm/), free to download on Google Play.
