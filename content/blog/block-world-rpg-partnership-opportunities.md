A block sandbox is built from parts: blocks, items, creatures, places. Parts like these give co-developed content somewhere to go. [Block World RPG Craft & Build](/apps/block-world-rpg/), titled Anime Craft — Build Block World inside the game, adds RPG progression and anime styling to the block-building formula. This page is for **character brands, art brands and publishers** exploring collaboration, and for anyone weighing a licence or purchase.

## Two halves of one game

The listing describes a **block-building sandbox combined with an RPG**. On the building side, players **gather resources, craft what they need and build a base**. On the adventuring side, they **explore, fight monsters, find dungeons and face dragons**, earning **EXP**.

The app's page on this site, drawing on the store screenshots, fills in the detail: **stats and skills**; **creative mode** with unlimited blocks and flight; **survival**, including a **peaceful** option; first-person combat and **archery**; tools and weapons; an inventory and backpack; a **quest journal**; a **world map**; **pets**; large worlds divided into regions; and multiple saved worlds. The art leans on **cherry blossoms and anime styling**. The game is free with ads, and Google Play lists in-app purchases.

## Content a partner could co-develop

Because the game is assembled from parts, collaboration can be precise:

| Element | Example collaboration |
| --- | --- |
| A region | A themed biome or landmark built around a brand's world |
| Blocks and decorations | A building set in a brand's visual style |
| Items | Tools or outfits with a partner's design |
| Pets | A companion creature based on a licensed character |
| Quests | A short questline in the journal tied to a campaign |

Each would be original content made with rights the partner holds, approved by both sides.

## Character and art brands

The game's audience, as the app's page on this site describes it, includes **fans of anime art and cherry-blossom scenery** and players who like **building first and fighting second**. A character brand with a gentle, decorative style could fit the **peaceful survival** and creative modes, where nothing threatens the player. An art brand could contribute a block palette or decoration set.

## Publishers

Publishers might discuss **publishing and distribution** in new markets, **localisation** of interface and quest text, **licensing**, **white-label versions where technically feasible**, **sponsorship** and **cross-promotion** with complementary games.

## Boundaries partners should know

Three points from the app's page on this site shape what a collaboration can promise:

- It is **an original Reign Creative game, not affiliated with any other block-building game or its publisher**. Partner material would not borrow another franchise's names, art or look.
- **The listing does not describe online multiplayer or shared worlds.** Collaborations would be single-player content, not live shared events.
- **Combat is part of the main survival loop**, so brands wanting a combat-free presence should target creative or peaceful play.

## A worked example: a spring festival region

An art brand proposes a cherry-blossom festival region for spring. The package: a small landmark with lanterns, a set of 20 decorative blocks, one festival pet and a three-step quest that sends players to find the landmark on the world map. It fits the game's existing art direction and works in creative and peaceful modes. Development scope, rights and the release window would be agreed in writing before work began, along with what happens to the region once the festival ends: whether it stays in players' saved worlds or becomes a seasonal return.

## Buying Block World RPG

Reign Creative LLC is open to serious discussions about **acquiring this game alone, with the other creative games, or as part of the portfolio**. Publicly, no **prices, valuations, revenue or player numbers** are given, and this page is not an offer.

## What a Google Play transfer involves

A new owner would receive the game through Google Play's **transfer between developer accounts**. Google's Play Console help covers the practical steps: both accounts registered and active, transaction IDs on the request, and an **active payments profile** on the receiving account because the game has in-app products. If the game uses services such as Google Play game services or Firebase, their settings move separately and must be updated. Users, ratings and the listing transfer; test groups and promotions do not.
