Reign Creative makes three hockey games for Android, and each asks something different of you: skate the shifts, make a player's decisions, or build the lineup. This comparison draws on their Google Play listings and their pages on this site, so you can choose by how you want to play.

## Hockey Career Pro League Game: play the shifts

The listing describes a hockey career played on the ice. **Career Single Control** puts you in your own role; **Team Control** directs the wider action. Off-puck play matters, following the puck and finding position, and each shift is graded **A to F** against coach objectives. Training and practice drills improve technique between games. [Its guide](/blog/hockey-career-pro-league-guide/) explains how to raise your grades, and [how long a hockey shift lasts](/blog/how-long-is-a-hockey-shift/) explains why each shift is so short.

## Hockey Career Sim 2027: the player's decisions

[Hockey Career Sim 2027](/apps/hockey-career-sim/) follows one player's journey rather than a front office. You pick a position and archetype, a scorer, a playmaker, a defensive leader or an elite netminder, and make the calls that move momentum: shoot, pass, defend, challenge the puck carrier or protect the net. Skating, shooting, passing, checking, positioning, stamina, awareness and goaltending develop through training, and roster spots have to be earned. Relationships with coaches, teammates, agents and media sit alongside injuries and contracts. It also supports offline play, so a season can continue without a connection. [The hockey career sim guide](/blog/hockey-career-sim-guide/) has more.

## 84-0 Hockey Draft & GM Manager: draft the six

[84-0 Hockey Draft & GM Manager](/apps/hockey-draft-gm-manager/) asks you to fill a **starting six**, a centre, both wings, two defence and a goaltender, then pick the coach who brings it together. The simulator projects a complete 84-game regular-season record; most rosters land short of perfect, so you draft again to beat your best. Unlimited Draft lets you keep searching, Limited Draft gives one pass with zero refreshes, and Franchise GM Mode turns a draft into a multi-season project. Quizzes, timed challenges, roster-building tasks, achievements and player comparisons fill in around the management. [Hockey line combinations explained](/blog/hockey-line-combinations-explained/) covers the balance it rewards.

## Choosing by how you want to play hockey

1. **You want to skate and be judged on every shift:** [Hockey Career Pro League Game](/apps/hockey-career-pro-league/).
2. **You want a career story, including the option to play in goal:** Hockey Career Sim 2027.
3. **You enjoy lineup puzzles and chasing a perfect record:** 84-0 Hockey Draft & GM Manager.

Each rewards a different kind of hockey knowledge. The action game rewards positioning, which [hockey positions explained](/blog/hockey-positions-explained/) describes; the career sim rewards choosing well at the points in a game where, as its page puts it, the decision actually matters; the draft game, its page says, rewards balance between scoring and defence and punishes a roster with thin spots.

## Moving between the three

The three share a sport but not a skill set. The action game's grades judge the job on each shift rather than the points; the draft game asks you to judge balance on paper before any game is projected; the career sim, according to its page, weights what you can attempt by the attributes you have trained. Starting with one and adding another changes which part of hockey you are thinking about: the shift, the season-long career, or the roster.

## What none of the three does

The action game takes practice before grades improve, as its page on this site notes. Hockey Career Sim 2027 always keeps you as the player, not a full front-office manager. The draft game's records are projections, and you do not play the games. None of the three is affiliated with any real league, team or players' association.
