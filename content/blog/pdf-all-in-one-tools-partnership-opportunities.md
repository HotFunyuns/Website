Paperwork on a phone is a series of small jobs: scan a receipt, sign a form, merge two files, shrink an attachment. [PDF All in One Tool No Limit](/apps/pdf-all-in-one-tools/) collects those jobs in one place. This page is for **productivity suites and document-workflow businesses** wondering whether a partnership could help their users, and for anyone interested in licensing or acquiring the app.

## Eighty-two tools in five groups

The listing describes a workspace it counts at **82 tools**, aimed at school notes, office reports, business forms, receipts, invoices, applications, contracts and personal records. They fall into five groups:

| Group | Listed tools |
| --- | --- |
| Annotate | Text, notes, highlights, drawings, marks and stamps |
| Pages | Rearrange, rotate, remove, extract, merge and split |
| Create and scan | PDFs from images, photos, text and other supported files; a scanner for paper pages |
| Forms | Complete fields, add text, sign |
| Send and store | Compress for email and messaging, organise and rename in a PDF manager, protection tools for sensitive documents |

It is free with ads, and Google Play lists in-app purchases.

## Productivity suites

For a suite that wants a mobile companion for quick edits, ideas Reign Creative LLC would discuss include **integrations supported by the app's existing capabilities**, such as opening a file in the app for a merge, a signature or compression, with anything beyond the current capabilities framed as new work; **distribution** arrangements; **cross-promotion**; and **co-development** of features both sides' users need.

## Document-workflow businesses

For firms that run onboarding, claims, applications or field paperwork, documents need to arrive complete and readable. A partner in that space might look at:

- **a guided capture flow**, using the scanner and compression so field staff send one small, legible PDF;
- **a white-label version where technically feasible**, carrying the business's brand;
- **localisation** for the markets the business operates in;
- **licensing** of the toolkit for use alongside its own systems.

## Honest descriptions of three features

Three features attract overstatement, so partner copy would stay close to the listing.

**Scanning.** Pages are **photographed with the phone camera**, so the app's page on this site notes that **lighting and focus affect the result**. A good guide tells users to scan flat, in even light.

**Signing.** The listing describes adding a signature and completing fields to prepare paperwork. It does not describe a certified e-signature service, and partners should not imply one.

**Protection.** The listing says protection tools **help manage sensitive documents**. That is not a guarantee of security, and claims would stay at that level.

## A worked example: the 10 MB attachment

A claims business asks customers to email a six-page form with three photographed receipts, and its mail system rejects attachments over 10 MB. A customer's raw scans come to 18 MB. In the app they merge the form and receipts into one file, then use the compress tools to try to bring it under the limit before sending; how far a file shrinks depends on its content. Each saved resend is time the business does not spend chasing missing documents. A partnership could formalise that flow into branded instructions with the business's own size limit built in, plus a short checklist: scan flat in good light, check every page is legible, merge into one file, compress, then send.

## What Google Play requires for a transfer

Google Play handles ownership changes through a **developer-account transfer**, and its Play Console help lists the main conditions. Both accounts must be registered and active, with transaction IDs supplied. For apps with in-app products, the receiving account needs an active payments profile. Translation projects using Google Play's translation service must be **completed before a transfer**. Afterwards, ad SDKs are updated, test groups are recreated, and the original account keeps its earnings reports. Google says its support team replies to approved requests **within 2 business days**.
