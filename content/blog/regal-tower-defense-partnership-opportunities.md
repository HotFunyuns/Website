[Regal Tower Defense: TD Game](/apps/regal-tower-defense/) is built around placement over raw damage; its Google Play listing promises "a tower game where placement matters". This page is for **strategy publishers** and **localisation partners**, and it sets out the campaign, the systems that make it distinctive, the partnership models that fit, and how a sale would be carried out.

## Ten worlds, fifty levels

The listing describes a **campaign of 50 levels across 10 worlds**:

| # | World | # | World |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Royal Meadow | 6 | Haunted Marsh |
| 2 | Stone Pass | 7 | Desert Citadel |
| 3 | Forest Bend | 8 | Crystal Caverns |
| 4 | Frozen Crossing | 9 | Celestial Gardens |
| 5 | Volcanic Gate | 10 | Chaos Realm |

Enemies include **goblins, brutes, wolves, trolls, drakes, golems, armoured units, flying units, elites and world bosses**, arriving in shifting combinations. Coins, gems, XP, stars, achievements, a **Tower Collection** and an enemy **Encyclopedia** run alongside. The game is free with ads, and Google Play lists in-app purchases.

## The three systems that set it apart

**Seven named attacking towers.** Arrow, Cannon, Frost, Flame, Lightning, Siege and Chaos each answer a different problem: armour, speed, resistance or crowding. The app's page on this site frames a lost wave as **the wrong tower in the right place, or the right tower in the wrong one**.

**Aura Towers.** Support towers that do not attack but **raise damage, attack speed, range or elemental power** of nearby towers, turning chokepoints into kill zones.

**Build Pads.** Purchasable positions placed **on valid terrain away from paths, towers, auras and the castle**, so the map is partly the player's design.

A usable **camera**, with pinch zoom, pan, saved presets and lead-enemy follow, keeps the layout readable.

## Strategy publishers

A publisher could look at **publishing and distribution**, **licensing**, **co-development** of an eleventh world or new tower types, and **white-label versions where technically feasible**. Because the campaign already has a defined difficulty curve across ten worlds, new content can be slotted in by difficulty rather than bolted on.

## Translating ten worlds and seven towers

This game's text is mostly names and short descriptions, and names matter: ten worlds, seven tower types, aura effects, enemy entries in the Encyclopedia and achievement descriptions. A localisation partner would also check that the Encyclopedia's descriptions stay accurate in translation, because players use them to plan.

## Why the Encyclopedia and Tower Collection help partners

Two collection systems run beside the campaign: a **Tower Collection** to expand and an **Encyclopedia** that fills in as players meet new enemies. For a co-development partner, they are ready-made places to introduce new content, since a new enemy gets an entry and a new tower joins the collection. That gives fresh content a home in the game's existing structure, and gives players a visible record of what they have discovered.

## Limits to keep in view

The listing describes a campaign and **does not mention multiplayer**. Ads support the free version and Google Play lists in-app purchases, so promotional copy should not suggest that progress depends on purchases.

## A worked example: scoping an eleventh world

If the campaign's 50 levels are spread evenly across its 10 worlds, each world holds 50 ÷ 10 = 5 levels. A co-developed eleventh world would therefore be scoped at five levels to match, introducing at least one new enemy combination and a world boss, and placed after the Chaos Realm on the difficulty curve. Balancing would cover all seven named attacking towers plus auras, so each new level is tested against eight tower roles.

## Acquiring Regal Tower Defense

Reign Creative LLC is open to serious discussions about **acquiring this game alone, both tower defence games together, or the portfolio**. This page does not include **prices, valuations, revenue or player numbers**, and it is not an offer to sell.

## Completing a sale on Google Play

The game would move to a buyer through Google Play's **developer-account transfer**. Google's help sets out the essentials: both accounts registered and active with transaction IDs, an active payments profile on the receiving account for in-app products, and updates to integrated services and **ad SDKs** after the move. Google adds that **orders created before the transfer stay with the original account**, that test groups must be recreated, and that its support team replies **within 2 business days** of an approved request.
