Cricket keeps score in round numbers. Fifty runs, a fifth wicket in an innings, a first-ball duck: each is a milestone with its own name, and Wikipedia's five-wicket-haul article credits Glenn McGrath with starting, in 2001, the tradition of a bowler raising the ball after taking five. This guide defines them, mostly from Wikipedia's statistics articles, and shows how they fit together.

## Batting milestones

| Milestone | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Half-century (fifty) | 50 or more runs in an innings |
| Century (hundred) | 100 or more runs in an innings |
| Double century | 200 to 299 runs |
| Triple century | 300 to 399 runs |
| Quadruple century | 400 to 499 runs |
| Century partnership | Two batters adding 100 runs together |

Wikipedia's century article notes that a double or triple century is still counted statistically as a century, so a player's career "hundreds" include them. Milestones are per innings, and per player: a team total of 100 is not a century for anyone.

A few landmarks from the same article give a sense of scale. W. G. Grace became the first batter to reach 100 first-class centuries in 1895; Jack Hobbs holds the first-class record with 199. The first ODI century was Dennis Amiss's 103 for England against Australia at Old Trafford on 24 August 1972. In Tests, Sachin Tendulkar holds the record with 51 centuries.

## Bowling milestones

| Milestone | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Five-wicket haul (5wI, "five-for", "fifer") | Five or more wickets by one bowler in an innings |
| Ten wickets in a match | Ten or more wickets by one bowler across a match's innings |
| Hat-trick | Three wickets in three consecutive deliveries by the same bowler |
| Maiden over | An over in which no runs are scored against the bowler |
| Wicket maiden | A maiden in which the bowler also takes a wicket |

Wikipedia's five-wicket-haul article calls taking five wickets a notable achievement, regarded as equivalent to a century. At Lord's, a century, five wickets in an innings or ten wickets in a match earns a place on the ground's honours boards. The same article records Muttiah Muralitharan of Sri Lanka with the most Test five-wicket hauls, 67, and Pakistan's Waqar Younis with the most in ODIs, 13. Pakistan's Naseem Shah became the youngest bowler to take one in a Test, in 2019, aged 16.

**Hat-tricks** have a precise definition: three wickets from three consecutive deliveries by the bowler, which may be interrupted by an over from the other end, or even by the other team's innings. Two wickets in two balls leaves a bowler "on a hat-trick". Only wickets credited to the bowler count, so a run out does not; [ways to get out](/blog/ways-to-get-out-in-cricket/) explains which dismissals those are.

**Maidens** depend on what the bowler is charged with. Wikipedia's over article explains that byes and leg byes can still be scored in a maiden, because they do not count against the bowler; [how cricket scoring works](/blog/how-cricket-scoring-works/) sets out which runs are charged to whom.

## The unwanted milestones

Wikipedia's duck article gives the vocabulary of failure:

| Term | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Duck | Dismissed without scoring |
| Golden duck | Dismissed by the first ball faced |
| Silver duck | Dismissed by the second ball faced, without scoring |
| Bronze duck | Dismissed by the third ball faced, without scoring |
| Pair (spectacles) | Dismissed for nought in both innings of the same two-innings match |
| King pair | Dismissed first ball in both innings, two golden ducks |

A batter who is not out on zero has not made a duck, because a duck requires a dismissal. Wikipedia explains that the pair takes its name from the two noughts together, which are thought to resemble a pair of spectacles. Even the greatest have them: Wikipedia records Don Bradman being dismissed for a first-ball duck in the second Test of the 1932–33 Ashes, on 30 December 1932.

## Milestones in the numbers

Milestones and averages measure different things. A batter can score several centuries and still average modestly if the other innings are short, while a consistent player may average 45 with few hundreds.
