[Football Career Soccer League](/apps/football-career-soccer-league/) is a soccer game about one thing: your career, played on the pitch. Its Google Play listing describes following a player in career mode, switching to team control for a wider view, or jumping into quick play when you only have a few minutes. In the game itself, the title reads **Soccer Career Pro League**, so if you see both names, they are the same app. This guide explains how it works and links to the soccer guides that help.

| Price | Free on Google Play; ads; no in-app purchases listed |

## Three ways to play

**Career mode** follows one player, whose skills develop across the league season.

**Team control** widens the view. Instead of following one player, you get a broader picture of the match, which suits players who like to direct the whole side.

**Quick play** is for short sessions, a single match without the career around it.

The store screenshots add a career hub with tiles for player career, team play, quick play, season and manager, and missions and rewards, so the season and the long-term goals are always one tap away.

## Which mode fits your session

The three modes trade time against control. A simple way to choose:

| If you have... | And you want... | Start with |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A few minutes | A shorter match | Quick play |
| A longer session | A player whose skills grow over the season | Career mode |
| Any amount of time | A wider view of the match than one player gives | Team control |
| Time between matches | To watch rather than play | Spectate |

The app's page lists the audiences it is built for: soccer fans who want to play matches rather than only manage them, players who like switching between one player and the whole team, anyone with a few minutes for a quick match, and people who follow their own numbers from game to game. If the last of those describes you, career mode and its match stats are the part to focus on.

## Playing the matches

The app's page on this site describes matches that are played rather than menu-driven. You find space, take players on and score goals, then check the **match stats** to set a target for the next game; the listing puts it as "Check match stats after a soccer match and set your next target." The camera can be adjusted for each match, and the store screenshots show a broadcast view and adjustable zoom.

If you want to understand the real game behind it, [how the offside rule works](/blog/how-the-offside-rule-works/) explains one of the laws that shapes attacking runs, and [expected goals](/blog/expected-goals-xg-explained/) explains how analysts measure shot quality in real matches.

## Growing a career

Player skills develop across the league season, and the listing describes daily rewards that give you another reason to return. Because the match stats come after every game, it can help to compare them across several matches rather than judge one. In real competitions, a league season is decided by a table; [how soccer league tables work](/blog/how-soccer-league-tables-work/) explains points, goal difference and tie-breakers.

You can also **spectate** matches: the listing describes watching the action with Spectate before your next match.

## A sensible first season

1. **Start in quick play** to learn the controls without career pressure.
2. **Try team control** once you are comfortable, for a wider view of the match.
3. **Adjust the camera** until you can follow play the way you like.
4. **Read the match stats after every game** and set one target for the next.
5. **Come back for daily rewards** between league games.
